title: Returns
---
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

To start a return, open Orders in your account and choose Return item. Without an account, contact support with your order id. We email you a prepaid return label.

Return shipping is free when the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item. For any other reason, $6.95 for the return label is deducted from your refund.

These items cannot be returned:
- items cut to length for you, such as chain, cable and hose;
- opened consumables, such as sanding discs and drill bits;
- gift cards.

We do not swap items directly. To exchange an item, return it and place a new order.

A used power tool that stops working is handled under the warranty, not as a return.
