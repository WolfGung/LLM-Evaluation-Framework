title: Tool care and storage
---
A few habits keep your tools working for years.

- Clean tools after use and dry them before storage.
- Wipe metal parts with a thin layer of light machine oil to prevent rust.
- Store tools in a dry place between 50 and 77 °F (10 and 25 °C).
- Keep saw blades and chisels sharp. A dull blade needs more force and is more dangerous.
- Never use a power tool with a damaged cord or a cracked housing.
- For long storage, keep lithium-ion batteries at 40 to 60 percent charge and recharge them every 3 months.

Rust caused by damp storage is not a defect and is not covered by the warranty.
