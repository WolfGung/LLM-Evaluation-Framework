title: Order status and cancellation
---
Toolshop order ids look like TS- followed by six digits, for example TS-104233.

To check an order, open Orders in your account, or enter your order id and email address on the order tracking page.

An order moves through these statuses:
- Received: we have your order and payment hold.
- Packed: the order is ready to leave the warehouse.
- Shipped: the parcel is with the carrier. We email you a tracking link.
- Delivered: the carrier has delivered the parcel.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled and nothing was charged.

You can cancel an order for free until its status is Packed. After that, wait for delivery and return the items.

If an order stays Received for more than 2 business days, contact support.
