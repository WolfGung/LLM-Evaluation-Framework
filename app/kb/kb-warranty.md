title: Warranty
---
Every tool from Toolshop has a warranty against defects in materials and workmanship:
- hand tools: lifetime warranty;
- power tools: 2 years from the delivery date;
- batteries and chargers: 1 year from the delivery date.

The warranty does not cover normal wear, misuse, accidents, rust from poor storage, or tools that were opened or modified by anyone other than an authorised service centre.

To make a claim, contact support with your order id, a short description of the problem and a photo or video. We email you a prepaid label. When the tool arrives, we repair or replace it within 10 business days. If we can neither repair nor replace it, we refund the price you paid.
