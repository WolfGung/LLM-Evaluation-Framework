title: Shipping costs and carriers
---
Toolshop ships to addresses in the United States and Canada.

- Standard shipping in the United States costs $5.95. It is free for orders over $75 before tax.
- Express shipping in the United States costs $14.95.
- Shipping to Canada costs a flat $24.95 per order.
- Items over 70 lb, such as table saws and air compressors, ship by freight for a flat $49. Freight cannot be delivered to a PO box.

We ship with UPS and USPS. Orders placed before 2 p.m. Eastern Time on a business day leave our warehouse the same day.

Lithium-ion batteries ship by ground only. An order with a battery cannot use express shipping.

You can change the delivery address until the order status is Packed. After that, the address cannot be changed.
