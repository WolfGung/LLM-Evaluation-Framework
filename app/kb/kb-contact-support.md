---
You can reach Toolshop support in three ways:
- email: support@example.com. We answer within 1 business day.
- phone: +1 555 0199, Monday to Friday 8:00 to 20:00 and Saturday 9:00 to 14:00 Eastern Time.
- chat: open Help in your account, during phone hours.

To help us answer quickly, include your order id if your question is about an order.

For a tool that smokes, sparks or overheats, stop using it, unplug it or remove the battery, and call us. Safety issues are handled first.
