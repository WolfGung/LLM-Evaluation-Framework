title: Batteries and chargers
---
Cordless tools use lithium-ion batteries. A battery works only with tools and chargers of the same brand and voltage platform, for example 18 V with 18 V.

Lithium-ion batteries ship by ground only, so an order with a battery cannot use express shipping.

Batteries and chargers have a 1-year warranty from the delivery date.

Never ship a swollen, cracked or leaking battery back to us. Stop using it, keep it away from heat, and contact support for safe disposal instructions.

You can bring old tool batteries to the counter at the Toolshop warehouse store. We recycle them for free, whatever the brand.
