title: Store pickup
---
You can choose free pickup at the Toolshop warehouse store, 1200 Example Avenue, Springfield. The store is open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 18:00.

In-stock items are ready 2 hours after you order during opening hours. We email you when the order is ready for pickup.

Bring your order id and a photo ID. Someone else can collect the order for you if they bring the order id and the ready-for-pickup email.

We hold a pickup order for 7 days. After that, it is cancelled and the payment hold is released.

Freight items can also be collected at the store. Our staff help you load them.
