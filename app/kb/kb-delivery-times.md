title: Delivery times
---
Delivery times are counted in business days (Monday to Friday, without public holidays) from the day the order ships.

- Standard shipping: 3 to 5 business days.
- Express shipping: 1 to 2 business days.
- Freight: 5 to 10 business days. The carrier calls you to book a delivery slot.
- Canada: 6 to 10 business days.

Your estimated delivery date is shown at checkout and in the shipping confirmation email.

If your parcel has not arrived 5 business days after the latest estimated date, contact support. We open a trace with the carrier. If the carrier confirms the parcel is lost, we send a replacement or give a full refund, whichever you prefer.
