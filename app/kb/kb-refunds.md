title: Refunds
---
We refund a return within 5 business days after it arrives at our warehouse and passes inspection. We email you when the refund is issued.

The refund goes to the original payment method. Your bank may need another 3 to 10 business days to show it on your statement. Orders paid with a Toolshop gift card are refunded to the gift card balance.

Original shipping costs are refunded only when the item was faulty or we sent the wrong item.

If a returned item is missing parts or is damaged by use, we may give a partial refund. We tell you the amount and the reason before we issue it.

If you cancel an order before it ships, the payment hold is released and nothing is charged.
