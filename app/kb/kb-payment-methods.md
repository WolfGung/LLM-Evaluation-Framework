title: Payment methods
---
Toolshop accepts these payment methods:
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover cards;
- PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay;
- Toolshop gift cards.

We do not accept cash on delivery or checks. All prices are in US dollars.

When you place an order, we put a hold on your card for the order total. The card is charged only when the order ships.

Business customers can apply to pay by invoice with 30-day terms. Approval takes up to 3 business days.

If your payment is declined, check that the billing address matches the address your bank has for the card, then try again or use another method.
